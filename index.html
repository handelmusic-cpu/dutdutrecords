About

Dut Dut Records is run by Ben Handel in Southern Indiana. The roster is a family affair: Sklermbot, Ben Handel, Quincy Handel and Jetson Handel, with room for more as the label grows. Everything is out digitally, from drifting electronic records to comedic songs and roots rock.
