ABOUT

DutDut Records is an independent label based in beautiful Southern Indiana, run by Ben Handel. The core roster spans four artists — Sklermbot, Ben Handel, Quincy Handel, and Jetson Handel — with dozens of releases so far, all out digitally, and room for more artists as the label grows. The sound ranges across electronic and experimental production, comedic songs, roots rock, and whatever else fits.
